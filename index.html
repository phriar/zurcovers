zurcovers

Your Long Box,
pulled from chain.

Paste a public Solana wallet address and step into your Long Box — every DC digital comic cover it holds, arranged like a shelf in a comic shop, with rarity and the current Magic Eden floor on the tag. No wallet connection, no signature, no transaction. Read-only, always.

ZurVault helps you find the books. ZurCovers lets you enjoy the collection.

Unofficial fan project — not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by DC Comics, Warner Bros. Discovery, or Candy Digital. It only ever reads a public wallet address you provide; it never requests a wallet connection and cannot move, sell, or modify anything you own.

Paste a public Solana wallet address to see the DC digital comic covers it holds — grid view with current Magic Eden floor prices, or a full-screen slideshow with NFT attributes. No wallet connection, no signature, no transaction. Read-only, always.

Unofficial fan project — not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by DC Comics, Warner Bros. Discovery, or Candy Digital. It only ever reads a public wallet address you provide; it never requests a wallet connection and cannot move, sell, or modify anything you own.

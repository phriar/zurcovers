A fan-built companion to zurvault.com — read-only tools for displaying the DC digital comic covers a Solana wallet already holds. No wallet connection, no signature, no transaction.

☐ What's here

zurvault.com is the original — a fan-built listings feed, collection browser, and price tracker for DC digital comic NFTs on Solana, pulling live data from Magic Eden and licensed Candy Digital drops. Browse, price-check, and find your next cover there.

zurcovers.com is where a wallet's own held covers come to life: paste a public address and Helius API key (session-only, never stored) to pull every DC cover that wallet holds, either as a static grid or as an auto-advancing fullscreen slideshow. It's currently DC-focused, same as zurvault — a Spawn/OddKey version may follow later.

Small/unfamiliar "collection" entries in a wallet are often unsolicited airdrop NFTs, not anything you acquired — Magic Eden simply not recognizing one is common and not itself a red flag. Never interact with anything claiming you need to "claim" a reward.
Never share your private key or seed phrase with anyone, including here. This page only ever needs a public wallet address — the same address anyone could already look up on a block explorer. It never asks for anything else.

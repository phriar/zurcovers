Collection Discovery

Scans your wallet's owned Spawn/OddKey comics, groups them by their on-chain collection, dedupes, and resolves each to a Magic Eden symbol — ready to paste into SPAWN_COLLECTIONS in zurcovers-proxy-worker.js. Same tool ZurVault uses, pointed at a different wallet and a different Worker.

Copy the array above into SPAWN_COLLECTIONS at the top of zurcovers-proxy-worker.js. Entries marked UNRESOLVED couldn't be matched to a Magic Eden symbol automatically — usually means it's not listed on ME, or the collection mint lookup failed. Fill those in by hand from the ME collection URL. As always: review every entry by hand before it goes live — not every "Spawn"-named thing you hold is necessarily a comic (could be a toy, a PFP, an unrelated art drop).
